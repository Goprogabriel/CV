Restore Rotate Pro

Open Rotate → Settings → Restore purchases while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. You can also restore from the subscription screen. Restoring purchases restores Pro access, not local routine history.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open your device’s Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Rotate. Deleting Rotate or using Start over does not cancel a subscription. Monthly and annual subscriptions provide the same Rotate Pro access.

Reminders

Choose reminders in Rotate and allow notifications when asked. If access was denied, enable notifications for Rotate in iOS Settings. Check Focus and sound settings. Reminders can be delayed or suppressed by the operating system.

Your records

Routine records are stored locally on your device. Export anything you want to keep before deleting the app. Check-ins and personal notes are optional in exports. Rotate has no account or server-side routine backup. Device backups depend on your operating-system settings.

Privacy and terms

Read the Rotate privacy policy and Apple standard Terms of Use.
