Your routines stay on your phone.

Rotate privacy policy · Effective 6 September 2026

Rotate is operated by Gabriel Hesthaven, Denmark. For privacy questions or requests, contact gaphbahe@gmail.com. No Rotate account is required.

Records on your device

Trackers, schedules, application and removal history, body locations, inventory, wellbeing check-ins, feelings, notes and selected photos are stored locally on your device. Rotate does not send these records to us or RevenueCat. We do not operate a server for your routine records and do not include advertising or health-data analytics.

Reminders are scheduled locally. Private notification text is enabled by default. If you choose to display routine names, those names may be visible on your lock screen.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes your payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and manages Rotate Pro access using a randomly generated app identifier, purchase and subscription history, and technical information such as app version, operating system, store region and currency. RevenueCat also provides subscription reports. We do not attach your routine records, health observations, photos, name or email to this identifier. We do not receive your payment card details.

Purchase information is used to deliver and restore subscription access, prevent fraud and administer subscriptions. It is not used for cross-app advertising. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy information.

Exports and backups

When you export or share records, the destination you choose receives that content and applies its own privacy practices. Check-ins and personal notes are excluded from exports unless you include them. Operating-system backups may contain local app files, depending on your device settings. Rotate does not provide cloud sync or a server-side backup.

Support and this website

If you contact support, we receive your message and return contact information. Email is handled using Gmail. Please avoid sending health records or personal photos unless necessary for your request. See Google’s privacy policy.

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to serve and secure the website. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by us. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Your local records remain until you delete them or remove the app’s storage. You can edit or delete supported records, export your data, or use Start over in Settings to clear tracking records. Start over preserves purchase identity and does not cancel a subscription. Deleting the app may delete local records; restoring purchases restores Pro access, not your records.

Support correspondence is kept for handling your request and necessary follow-up or legal requirements. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat for subscription administration, purchase restoration, fraud prevention and applicable legal obligations. Deleting the app does not automatically erase those providers’ records. Contact us to request deletion of information we control.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact gaphbahe@gmail.com; we cannot retrieve routine records from your phone.

For EEA users, subscription administration is based on delivering the service you request; support and protection against fraud are based on our legitimate interests in operating a reliable service, and legally required records are processed to meet legal obligations. Service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, subject to their applicable data-transfer safeguards. Their policies above provide further details.

You can complain to your local data-protection authority, including Datatilsynet in Denmark.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if our practices change.
